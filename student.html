About Us – Muhanga Technical Center (MTC)

Muhanga Technical Center (MTC) ni ishuri ry’imyuga n’ubumenyingiro (TVET) riherereye mu Karere ka Muhanga, rigamije guteza imbere ubumenyi bufatika n’ubushobozi bwifashishwa ku isoko ry’umurimo.

Dutanga amahugurwa mu mashami atandukanye arimo SOD, NIT, AUT, BDC, na FBO, twigisha mu buryo bujyanye n’ikoranabuhanga n’iterambere rigezweho.

Intego ya MTC ni gutoza abahanga, gufasha urubyiruko kwihangira imirimo, no guteza imbere iterambere rirambye ry’igihugu binyuze mu burezi bufite ireme.

MTC yiyemeje gutanga uburezi bufite ireme, bushingiye ku masomo afatika, indangagaciro nziza, n’ubufatanye n’abandi bafatanyabikorwa.

Our Mission

Empowering students, teachers, and schools in Rwanda with an easy-to-use digital learning platform, ensuring quality education and transparent reporting.

🛠️ Services Dutanga – MTC

Muhanga Technical Center (MTC) itanga serivisi zitandukanye zigamije guteza imbere ubumenyi n’ubushobozi bw’abanyeshuri n’abaturage muri rusange, zirimo:

  • Amahugurwa y’imyuga n’ubumenyingiro (TVET) mu mashami atandukanye (SOD, NIT, AUT, BDC, FBO)
  • Amasomo afatika (practical training) afasha abanyeshuri kubona ubumenyi bukoreshwa ku kazi
  • Kwigisha ikoranabuhanga n’imikoreshereze ya mudasobwa
  • Gufasha abanyeshuri kwihangira imirimo n’ubujyanama mu bucuruzi
  • Amahugurwa magufi (short courses) ku rubyiruko n’abakuze
  • Guhuza abanyeshuri n’amasoko y’umurimo (internship & job orientation)
  • Kugira inama no gutoza imyuga ku baturage (community outreach)
